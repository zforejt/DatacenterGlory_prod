How going public works

%%S1%%

1. Earn money. Everything you earn in a region in this run counts toward its Golden Server awards. The more you earn, the more awards.

%%S2%%

2. Go public. Sell the region's company on the stock exchange. That region starts again from its first location. Your other regions stay as they are.

%%S3%%

3. Earn more, forever. Every award gives +2% income in its region in all future runs. Upgrades and managers get dearer too, but much less than your income grows, and locations keep their price: every run goes faster than the last.

Tap the award button at the top to go public. Awards are never spent. A new region opens with a licence: buy it on the Map with the money of the region before. Every region has its own currency.
